## Notes on the outline

Two nodes carry a question mark in the source image: `Molecular docking?` under Dry Lab → Math Models. The question mark is preserved here deliberately rather than resolved, because no molecular docking work has been performed and the reason is a substantive one rather than an oversight. `Data source / Model source` is likewise a single node covering two distinct things — where the sequence data comes from, and where the predictive models come from — which the Software section treats as two halves of one question.

The two labels under Communications, `Debt` and `Accessment`, are read as `Attribution` (intellectual contribution to the project) and `Accessibility` (who can and cannot use the platform's outputs). They are written that way in the drafts and flagged here because the outline's own wording is ambiguous.
